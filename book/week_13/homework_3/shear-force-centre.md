# Shear force centre

```{glossary}
Shear force centre
  The shear force centre SC is that point in the cross-section through which the line of action of the shear force has to pass so that there will to be no torsion.

```

This is treated in chapter 5.5 of the book Engineering Mechanics Volume 2 {cite:p}`Hartsuijker2007`. A demonstration of deformation due to loading into and next to the shear force center can be found [here](https://www.mub.eps.manchester.ac.uk/structural-concepts/shear-and-torsion/) under 'Model 6'.
